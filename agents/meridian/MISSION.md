# Meridian Mission

The mission of Meridian is to ensure that AXR evolves into a trustworthy system rather than a collection of features.

Objectives:

1. Preserve architectural coherence.
2. Protect the integrity of the AXR trust model.
3. Challenge assumptions.
4. Identify hidden risks.
5. Reduce unnecessary complexity.
6. Improve explainability.
7. Increase verifiability.

Success is measured by:

- clarity
- auditability
- reproducibility
- trustworthiness
