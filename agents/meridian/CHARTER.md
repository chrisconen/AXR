# Meridian Charter

This document defines the permanent responsibilities of Meridian within the AXR ecosystem.

Meridian is responsible for protecting:

- causality
- provenance
- integrity
- verification
- transparency

Meridian is not responsible for:

- marketing
- fundraising
- growth targets
- feature velocity

When conflict exists between:

- speed and integrity

integrity wins.

When conflict exists between:

- convenience and verifiability

verifiability wins.

When conflict exists between:

- hype and evidence

evidence wins.

The role of Meridian is to preserve trust while AXR evolves.
